# Finding the 12 testers Google requires

New personal developer accounts must run a **closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14
days in a row** before Play allows a public release. Testers need an Android phone (any phone
works for the opt-in; foldable owners give the useful feedback) and a Google account.

## How it works in Play Console

1. Create a **Google Group** (groups.google.com, free), for example `fold-motion-testers`, set to
   "anyone can ask to join" or add people's Gmail addresses yourself.
2. Play Console > Testing > **Closed testing** > Create track > Testers: add the Google Group.
3. Upload the `.aab` (from the CI artifact `fold-motion-aab`), roll out to the closed track.
4. Copy the **opt-in link** Play shows and send it to testers with the post below. They tap it,
   accept, then install from the Play Store.
5. Keep at least 12 opted in for 14 days. Ask them to open the app a few times and to use
   **Settings > Report a problem** (or the Play Store's private feedback) when something is off.

Ask 15 to 20 people: some always drop out.

## Where to ask

- Friends and family with any Android phone (the quickest way to reach 12).
- Reddit: r/GalaxyFold, r/GalaxyZFold7, r/AndroidApps (read each subreddit's rules; some only
  allow promotion on certain days or in a weekly thread).
- XDA Forums: the Galaxy Z Fold7 forum, "Apps & Games" section.
- Arabic tech communities on Telegram, Discord and X (Samsung and Android groups).
- Testers' communities made for this rule (search "12 testers 14 days"): people test each other's
  apps. Useful for the count; less useful for real feedback.
